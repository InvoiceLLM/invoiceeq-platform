## Suggested demo path (uses `showcase/vpi_demo`)

1. **Agentic AI** — upload the 11 inbound + 9 outbound PDFs, then the 6 image formats. NOVA extracts; SENTINEL flags the two planted duplicates and the planted math error.
2. **Automation** — email one invoice to the app mailbox; show SSE ledger and completion email.
3. **Business Intelligence** — attach a PO and the bank statement in chat; insight bubble appears before any question. Ask SAGE for unpaid inbound after the bank statement and open the SQL audit drawer.
4. **AI Eval & Observability** — open the AI Control Tower workbook: nightly benchmark scores, judge verdicts, LLM cost by tool.
5. **Development Harness** — show a Gap entry, its spec section, and the real-Postgres test evidence that closed it.
