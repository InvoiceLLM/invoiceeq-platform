Arithmetic verification Regex rules against extracted strings; breaks on layout shifts or character confusion (O vs. 0). No independent rebuild of totals. Model generates the most probable total. When line items and tax don't reconcile, it produces a figure that makes the arithmetic look right — and never signals uncertainty. Exact decimal engine in deterministic code. No model in the arithmetic path. A total that cannot be rebuilt from line items stops the invoice — always.
Three-way reconciliation Priced add-on in most systems. Matching is rigid exact-string — part number spelling variations and partial deliveries create manual exceptions at scale. Unreliable across multi-table line items. Model context windows struggle with long PO line lists; partial delivery logic is inconsistent across invocations. Three-tier cascade: exact match, fuzzy string, vector similarity on part descriptions. Partial deliveries and price variance bands handled natively within ±0.01 tolerance.
Rule learning & adaptation Consultant engagement required per template change. A supplier who redesigns their PDF triggers a project, not a correction. Prompt drift — a tuning change for one supplier pattern silently degrades accuracy for another. No replay corpus, no regression gate. Every candidate rule replayed against 500 historical invoices before promotion. Zero regressions on record. Operators define rules through the UI — no developer required.
Data isolation & retention Typically on-premise or private-cloud deployments with large footprints. Data isolation is physical, but the operational cost is high. Training and retention exposure. General-purpose API providers may use inputs to improve base models unless enterprise agreements explicitly prevent it — and those agreements are not universal. Zero retention for training. Per-tenant cryptographic isolation at the storage layer — partitioned vector indices, row-level database predicates, ephemeral signed URLs. Not configurable out of this state.
Time to production 4–9 month consulting cycle typical. Template creation, integration, UAT, and rollout per supplier cluster are sequential. Bespoke internal engineering project. Prompt engineering, validation tooling, and exception handling are not packaged — each deployment is built. 14 days to production cutover on a standard ERP with credentials available. Caveat: custom connectors and multi-entity rollouts extend this — see the deployment page.

Evaluation scorecard

12 criteria. Score us and
anyone else on the same sheet.

Use these criteria to evaluate InvoiceEQ and any competitor. The criteria are designed so that a system that scores well on all 12 actually works in production — not one that passed a demo on a clean sample.

1
Arithmetic reconstruction rate on your worst documents

Run the same 500 documents through every system. What percentage of totals does each system reconstruct correctly from line items — independently, not by reading the stated total?

2
Duplicate detection rate on same-vendor, same-total, different-number invoices

Layer-3 near-duplicates — the ones where the fraud or error is a changed invoice number, not a changed amount — are the most expensive class to miss.

3
STP rate on partial-delivery invoices

Take 50 invoices where the billed quantity is less than the PO quantity. What fraction process straight through without a false exception?

4
Regression rate on rule changes

After tuning a rule for one supplier, what fraction of other suppliers' invoices extract worse than before? The number should be zero.

5
Time to process a layout-changed invoice without a manual rule update

A supplier moves their total to a different position on the page. How long does it take the system to handle the change — minutes, days, or a consulting engagement?

6
Cross-tenant data read test

Authenticate as Tenant A. Attempt a direct database query or API call referencing a known Tenant B invoice ID. What is returned?

7
Audit trail completeness for a randomly chosen processed invoice

Pick any processed invoice at random. Can you reconstruct: what was extracted, which rule applied, which PO it matched, who reviewed it, and when?

8
Time-to-baseline on a new entity onboard

Adding a new legal entity: is it a configuration (hours) or a migration project (weeks)?

9
Total coverage at any invoice volume

Does the system check every invoice at every value — including the $42 freight invoice that nobody reads — or does it sample?

10
Natural-language query fidelity on your own invoice history

Ask: "How much did we pay [top supplier] in the last 90 days, broken down by month?" Is the answer sourced and verifiable, or does it read as a confident estimate?

11
Outbound invoice error rate before customer delivery

Run your last 200 outbound customer invoices through pre-flight checks. How many carry a line item or tax error that would trigger a customer dispute?

12
What the vendor says when you ask where it doesn't apply

Ask every vendor: "When is your product the wrong choice?" A vendor who can answer this clearly is a vendor who understands their own product's limits.
