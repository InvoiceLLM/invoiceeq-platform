Under "Extra information required in some cases", the Commission's invoicing
page states that where the customer is liable for the tax — the reverse-charge
procedure — the document must carry the words "reverse charge".

We flag a document that charges no tax at all and does not say why. That is not
a finding on its own: zero tax can be right for several reasons, and the check
tells you the wording is missing, not that the tax treatment is wrong.
