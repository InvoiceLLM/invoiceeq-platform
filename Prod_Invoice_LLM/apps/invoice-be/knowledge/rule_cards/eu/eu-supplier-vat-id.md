The Commission's list of what a full VAT invoice must contain includes the
"Supplier's VAT identification number", alongside the date of issue, a unique
sequential number, both parties' names and addresses, and the VAT rate and
amount payable.

A document treated as an invoice — which includes a credit or debit note —
carries the same identification. We check that a VAT identification number in a
recognised EU format appears somewhere on the document.
