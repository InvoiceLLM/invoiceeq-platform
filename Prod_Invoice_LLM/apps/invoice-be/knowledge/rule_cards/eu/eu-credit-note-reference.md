The Commission's invoicing page lists, among the details a VAT invoice must
carry, a "specific, unambiguous reference to the initial invoice and the details
that are being amended (on a credit note, debit note or other document treated
as an invoice)".

In plain terms: a credit or debit note that does not say which bill it adjusts
cannot be matched to that bill by anyone — us, your accountant or a tax officer.
We check that the note names at least one document number.
