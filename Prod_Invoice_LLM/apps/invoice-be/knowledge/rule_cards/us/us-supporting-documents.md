IRS Publication 583 says: "Purchases, sales, payroll, and other transactions you
have in your business generate supporting documents. Supporting documents
include sales slips, paid bills, invoices, receipts, deposit slips, and canceled
checks. These documents contain information you need to record in your books. It
is important to keep these documents because they support the entries in your
books and on your tax return."

There is no federal rule about what a purchase order or delivery note must
contain — that is a matter of state law and of your own contract. So this card
is guidance, not a check: keep the document, filed by year and type.
