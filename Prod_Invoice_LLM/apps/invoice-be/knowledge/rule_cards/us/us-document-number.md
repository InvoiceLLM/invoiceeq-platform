Publication 583 requires supporting documents to be kept in an orderly fashion,
organised "by year and type of income or expense", because they support the
entries in your books.

A document with no number of its own cannot be referred to from a book entry, or
from any other document. This check is the practical form of that requirement,
not a rule quoted from the tax code: we flag a document that prints no
identifier.
