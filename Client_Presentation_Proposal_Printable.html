7. Phase 2 — Full Integration Roadmap

Phase 2 extends the core MVP into a fully connected enterprise platform — linking your existing ERP systems and teaching the AI engine your specific invoice layouts for permanent accuracy gains.

Screen A — Integrations & Settings

✅ Live ERP Connectors

Salesforce, SAP S/4HANA, and QuickBooks sync with live status and invoice pull counts.

✅ Global Configuration

Toggle auto-approvals, anomaly alerts, dual-auditor rules, and batch-size limits in one panel.

✅ Ledger Export Triggers

Push approved invoice summaries directly back into connected ERP ledger entries.

Screen B — Template Learning Registry

✅ Vendor-Specific Rules

Bind corrected OCR offsets to vendor layouts — future matching invoices skip AI steps entirely.

✅ Accuracy Tracking

Each template card tracks measured accuracy improvement (avg. +15%) over baseline AI extraction.

✅ Tag-Based Categorisation

Templates tagged #IT, #Contractors, #General are auto-applied on future matching uploads.
